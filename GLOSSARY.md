# Domain Glossary — sea_of_learning

This glossary establishes the canonical domain vocabulary for the **sea_of_learning** educational study platform.

---

## Core Domain Concepts

### Track
A structured, top-level field of academic study (e.g., Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics, Literature). A Track organizes related Courses and Topics into a pedagogical path.

### Course / Topic
A focused academic subject within a Track (e.g., Calculus, Quantum Mechanics, Machine Learning). Groups a sequence of modular Lectures.

### Lecture
A single self-contained educational video lesson. Every Lecture possesses:
- An academic category tag
- Title, syllabus description, instructor attribution
- Precise duration and milestone metadata
- A zero-ad study environment

### Curriculum Pathway
A pedagogical sequence of Lectures organized by prerequisite dependencies rather than arbitrary popularity rankings.

### Study Session
A student's active learning timeframe, categorized by duration brackets:
- **Bite-Sized**: 15 minutes or less
- **Standard Lecture**: 15 to 45 minutes
- **Deep Dive**: 45 minutes or more

### Milestone
A critical concept or learning objective within a Course that signifies mastery before unlocking subsequent lectures.

### Study Note / Discussion Note
A student-contributed reflection, question, or timestamped annotation attached to a Lecture.
